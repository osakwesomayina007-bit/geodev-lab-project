# Month 1 Summary

## 1. Question
Which areas of Eti-Osa have limited access to parks, sports facilities and other recreational spaces?

## 2. Spatial operation
A 1 km proximity-based accessibility analysis was performed. Recreational facilities were grouped by geometry, buffered by 1 km, merged and dissolved. The resulting accessibility area was clipped to the Eti-Osa study area, and a Difference operation was used to identify areas outside the 1 km accessibility zone.

## 3. What I expected, and what I got
I expected areas around mapped recreational facilities to fall within the accessibility zone, while areas farther away would be identified as limited-access areas.

## 4. What surprised me
I was surprised by how unevenly recreational facilities are distributed across Eti-Osa. While large areas fall within 1 km of mapped recreational facilities, some sizeable areas remain outside the accessibility zone, showing that recreational facilities are not evenly distributed across the study area.

## 5. What data I still need
A settlement and/or population dataset would help identify the specific communities and populations located within the limited-access areas.
